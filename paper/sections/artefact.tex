\section{Reproducibility}
\label{sec:artefact}

The tables and figures in this paper are generated from saved outputs rather
than transcribed by hand. Training runs write their own metric history, each
sweep writes a JSON record of every level and threshold, and the smartphone
benchmark writes a report that is pulled from the device. Non-regenerable
measurements, particularly smartphone reports, are stored under unique
filenames and never overwritten. The one such loss that occurred during this
work is reported beside the measurements it affects.

The companion iOS application bundles the exported ladder, exposes the model
and compute-unit selection used in Section~\ref{sec:ladder}, and runs the
benchmark unattended. The reported device measurements therefore come from the
same build that a user would install.
