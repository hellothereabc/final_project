## Ireland funding note

The 2026--2028 IReL--Elsevier agreement covers open-access APCs for eligible
corresponding authors at participating Irish institutions in participating
hybrid and Gold journals. The annual allocation is limited to 1,450 papers and
IReL warned in July 2026 that the 2026 quota might be exhausted before year end.
Check both the institution and exact journal in the agreement before submission;
do not assume that an Elsevier APC will automatically be covered.

## Submission checklist

- Confirm scope using the final title and abstract, not just keywords.
- Check indexing and the latest JIF/CiteScore on the publisher page.
- Verify APC, VAT, institutional agreements, waivers, and whether a no-APC
  subscription route exists.
- Check page/word limits, data and code policies, and required declarations.
- Confirm that dataset licences allow the planned figures and repository links.
- Check whether prior conference publication affects a later journal version.
- Budget mandatory conference registration, travel, accommodation, and visa.
- Never submit simultaneously to two venues.
